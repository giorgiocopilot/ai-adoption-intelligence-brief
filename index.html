What I see in the corporate field

Three AI tensions that shape real adoption.

01 · Overhyped

People hear “a Nobel Prize in your pocket”, then cannot make AI work in the flow of a normal day.

02 · Underestimated

Businesses pay for AI, but many have barely any users, no role journey and no evidence that work is changing.

03 · Overwhelming

New models, new tools and a new paradigm arrive continuously. Employees need clarity, not another flood of announcements.

Giorgio’s practical position

Make AI useful before making it grand.

Giorgio works with people who want to benefit from AI, not become an overnight “AI company”. The operating model creates safe, role-relevant habits and makes the value visible to leaders.

Enterprise first 90 days

Establish the measurement and intervention system before rollout waves scale.

A proposed starting framework to tailor with client sponsors once scope, regulatory context, target cohorts and governance model are clarified.

Days 0–30

Baseline and mobilise

Define success measures, employee personas, change impacts, governance cadence, data definitions and the stakeholder network.

Outcome: shared definition of success before scale begins.
Days 31–60

Activate priority cohorts

Design role-based journeys, a leader toolkit, champion model, communications, pilot enablement and feedback routes.

Outcome: early evidence of what creates sustained use.
Days 61–90

Scale and prove

Introduce segmentation, benchmark views, targeted interventions, executive value review and a scale roadmap.

Outcome: investment decisions based on adoption intelligence.
